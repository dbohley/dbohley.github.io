Integrated Respiratory Sensing and Gated Control for Autonomous Needle Insertion via CT Bore Robotic Arm

Emma Huang*, Moira Bohley*, Krishna Jamakhandi, Rebecca Fellbaum, Changwei Chen, Derek Chen, Ananya Rao, Sean Tutton, Albert Hsiao, Elizabeth Peiros, Michael C. Yip

* Equal contribution

Read the paper (arXiv)

Overview

CT-guided needle procedures such as biopsies and ablations need the needle to reach a target that moves every time the patient breathes. We put a robotic arm inside the CT bore, sense the patient's breathing in real time, and gate the insertion so the needle advances only during a safe, repeatable window of the breathing cycle. Bi-manual Stabilization of the Cervical Spine for Safe Physical Human-Robot Interaction in Rolling Maneuvers

Elizabeth Peiros, Moira Bohley, Lucas Yager, Xiao Liang, Derek Chen, Rebecca Fellbaum, Chris D'Ambrosia, Ananya Rao, Michael C. Yip

Read the paper (arXiv)

Overview

When an injured person has to be rolled, for example during casualty extraction, their head and neck must stay in line with the spine to avoid worsening a possible spinal injury. This usually takes trained responders. We developed a two-arm controller that stabilizes the cervical spine through the roll and demonstrated it on a Unitree G1 humanoid. [Add the headline result.]
